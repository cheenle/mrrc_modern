05. Non-Functional Requirements (ART 0507)

Requirements here are numbers that can be measured on this board, and every row carries the method that would show the requirement has been broken. Per chapter 00 §V1, each target also names its scenario: a throughput figure without its window is a rumour, not a requirement.

Status vocabulary (chapter 00 §V5): field-exercised means measured on the real unit over a sustained run; implemented means measured on the bench only; proposed means no measurement exists yet and the row must say so.
